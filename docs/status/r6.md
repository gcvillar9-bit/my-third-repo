# R6 polish pass (owner request: "make sure the game is perfect, add what is missing")

Work: `claude/gracious-thompson-yyfnwf`. Method: every bookmark rendered and looked at (88 views), the full e2e
suite as a baseline, then fixes and features. Docs: `docs/modules/ui.md`, `engine.md`, `tours.md`.

## Defects found and fixed

- Io drew as a black disc with glowing dots and Titan's ground was black: the texture build multiplied both
  colour maps by an alpha mask (`tools/lib/image.mjs`); maps regenerated, guarded by unit tests.
- Mars, Earth and the deep-time Earth showed their mesh grid through the atmosphere on potato and low: flat
  facets sag 1-2 km below the sphere. New chunk `src/render/chunks/facet.glsl`, used by spheres, coarse terrain
  tiles and the cloud shell.
- Dynamic resolution never engaged below about 4 fps (every frame counted as a hitch).
- The quality preset was forgotten on reload.
- Star and black-hole names over a daylit sky; six far black holes named in every view; labels cut by the
  right edge; test bookmarks offered in search; a material warning on every boot.

## Added

- Loading screen, boot and WebGL failure messages, context-loss recovery, automatic quality.
- Explore panel (about 100 places by theme), tours menu and four more tours, controls panel with credits.
- Click or tap to select, Land / Walk / Take off, latitude and longitude in the HUD, orbit-line and label
  switches, full screen, Home.
- Touch: look, pinch to fly, on-screen stick; phone layouts. Eased flight.
- Descriptions on all 42 Solar System info cards.
- `tests/e2e/shell.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/sweep.spec.ts` (every bookmark, run before a release).

## Gaps

- Not checked on a real GPU or a real phone: only software rendering was available here.
- No gamepad support; no pointer-lock look; tours have captions but no narration.
- Orbit lines are drawn at a fixed brightness, so they stand out in very dark scenes (backlit Saturn).
