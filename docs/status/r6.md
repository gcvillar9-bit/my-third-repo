# R6 polish pass (owner request: "make sure the game is perfect, add what is missing")

Work: `claude/gracious-thompson-yyfnwf`. Method: every bookmark rendered and looked at (88 views), the full e2e
suite as a baseline, then fixes and features. Docs: `docs/modules/ui.md`, `engine.md`, `tours.md`.

## Defects found and fixed

- Io drew as a black disc with glowing dots and Titan's ground was black (colour maps multiplied by an alpha
  mask in `tools/lib/image.mjs`); regenerated, with unit tests.
- Mars, Earth and the deep-time Earth showed their mesh grid through the atmosphere on potato and low (flat
  facets sag 1-2 km): `src/render/chunks/facet.glsl` for spheres, coarse terrain tiles and the cloud shell.
- Dynamic resolution never engaged below about 4 fps; the quality preset was forgotten on reload; a bookmark's
  narrow field of view stayed on afterwards; label clutter (stars in daylight, far black holes, cut-off labels).
- CI: the smoke job could not install its browser on the new `ubuntu-latest`; pinned to ubuntu-24.04.

## Added

- Loading screen, failure messages, context-loss recovery, automatic and remembered quality.
- Explore (about 110 places by theme), five tours, controls panel with credits, click to select, Land / Walk /
  Take off, position readout, orbit-line and label switches, full screen, Home.
- Touch (look, pinch, stick, phone layouts), gamepad, eased flight; descriptions on 42 info cards.
- Modules `comets` and `spacecraft` (their own docs), built in parallel and reviewed here.
- An independent review gave 16 findings, all fixed. E2E `shell.spec.ts`, `sweep.spec.ts` (every bookmark).

## Gaps

- Not tried on a real GPU, phone or gamepad: only software rendering was available here.
- No pointer-lock look; tours have captions but no narration.
- Orbit lines are drawn at a fixed brightness, so they stand out in very dark scenes (backlit Saturn).
